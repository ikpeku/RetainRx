# RetainRx

> Helping independent pharmacies keep the people they already serve, and proving, in naira, that it worked.

**Capture · Remember · Remind · Reserve · Return · Learn.** One service, three touchpoints, real outcomes.

| Touchpoint | Who | Cost to them |
|---|---|---|
| **Rexa** on WhatsApp | Customers | Free |
| **Counter Panel** (tablet, no login) | Counter attendants | Free |
| **Owner Workspace** (+ Clinical page) | Pharmacy owner (pays) · Superintendent pharmacist | Owner pays |
| **Admin console** | Founders & support | — |
